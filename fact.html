5 FACTS ABOUT MARTIN CORTIS

Leader and Tallest Member

Born on March 20, 2008, Martin stands at an impressive 193 cm (approx. 6'4"), making him the tallest member of CORTIS.

Pre-Debut Producer Credits

Before debuting, he trained for 6 years and contributed to songwriting and producing for senior HYBE artists, including tracks like ILLIT's "Magnetic" and TXT's "Deja Vu".

2018 Winter Olympics Performance

As a child, Martin was a member of the Rainbow Children's Choir and performed the national anthem at the 2018 PyeongChang Winter Olympics opening ceremony.

Korean-Canadian Heritage

He was born in Seoul but is of Korean-Canadian descent, having grown up in Ottawa, Canada, and is fully bilingual in English and Korean.

Unpopular Food Opinions

Among the CORTIS members, Martin is well-known for being the only one who genuinely enjoys mint chocolate and Hawaiian pizza (pineapple on pizza).
